A mascot-based mobile app that helps first-time dog owners manage routines and connect with other pet owners.

DisemBARK

This project focuses on designing a mobile app for first-time dog owners after the adoption process. The app helps users manage pet care routines, reduce stress, and build connections with other dog owners through social features.

Problem

First-time dog owners often struggle after adoption due to lack of guidance, new responsibilities, and changes in daily routines. This can cause stress, uncertainty, and difficulty maintaining consistent care.
Another problem is the lack of community support, which can make users feel alone and unsure about their decisions.

Solution

We designed a mascot-based task manager app with social features. Users can create custom routines that turn into tasks, helping them manage daily pet care.
The app includes features like photo-based task completion, where users take photos to complete tasks and save them as memories. A reward system with a customizable mascot helps motivate users to stay consistent.
The app also includes community features, allowing users to connect with others, share profiles, and interact through friend codes or nearby connections. The design focuses on guidance, motivation, and reducing stress for new dog owners.

Process

We started with research including articles, social media, observations, and interviews to understand user needs and challenges. At first, we explored the adoption process, but after feedback, we shifted focus to post-adoption where users struggle the most.
We created early wireframes and tested different concepts before choosing a mascot-based routine manager. Then we built prototypes and conducted usability testing with 5 participants.
One challenge was navigation—users had difficulty starting tasks and understanding icons. We improved this by adding clearer labels, better onboarding, and refining navigation. Another issue was that users needed more guidance, so we added more instructions and simplified flows.
Through iteration, we improved layout, interaction, and overall usability. The final design includes clear navigation, connected features, and a more user-friendly experience.
