Custom Chimera

This project focuses on creating a chimera using Photoshop by combining at least three real animals into one believable creature. The final design is presented as a creative advertisement with a name, story, and character traits.

Problem

The challenge is to create a believable creature by blending different animals together. The body, textures, lighting, and colours must look natural and consistent.

Another problem is to avoid simply placing parts together, but instead fully integrate them into one form. The advertisement also needs to match the story and purpose of the creature.

Solution

I created the Black Tortoise (Xuanwu) by combining a turtle (body and shell), snake (tail and body), and lizard (head). The design is based on a mythological guardian creature that represents strength, protection, and longevity.

I used Photoshop techniques like masking, cloning, and blending to make the creature look realistic and fully integrated. I also designed an advertisement with a strong headline, background, and layout to support the story and make the creature feel believable.

Process

I started by researching the Black Tortoise myth and gathering high-quality images of turtle, snake, and lizard. Then I planned the composition and created rough sketches.

In Photoshop, I combined the animals using non-destructive editing such as layer masks, blending, and adjustment layers. I focused on matching lighting, colour, and texture to make the creature look natural.

One challenge was blending different textures (shell, scales, skin), which I solved by adjusting colours and smoothing transitions. Another challenge was creating a realistic water reflection, which required adjusting opacity, distortion, and blur to match the environment.

I also worked on keeping the anatomy believable by refining proportions and details. After creating the creature, I placed it into a background and designed a final advertisement with text and layout. All files were organized into a packaged folder with PSD, images, and assets.
